Given Topic: The field of large vision-language model safety focuses on defending against malicious visual inputs that induce harmful generation. Current input-side defense paradigms rely on semantic alignment and safety prefix injection; however, they exhibit two critical deficiencies. First, global feature matching fails to localize harmful semantics within complex visual scenes, causing ambiguous detection boundaries between safe and unsafe content. Second, safety signals from prepended textual guidance progressively attenuate during cross-modal autoregressive decoding, resulting in inconsistent refusal behaviors where initial safeguards are overridden. The core problem is to establish a robust inference-time defense that achieves fine-grained malicious content identification while ensuring persistent activation of the model's latent safety mechanisms throughout the entire generation trajectory.
