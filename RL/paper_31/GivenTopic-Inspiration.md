Given Topic: Offline-to-online reinforcement learning aims to enhance offline pretrained agents through online fine-tuning, yet existing approaches predominantly address Q-value estimation bias under assumptions of finite variance or Gaussianity. Empirical evidence indicates that during online fine-tuning, the distributional shift between offline and online data induces Q-value estimation biases that exhibit heavy-tailed characteristics. Such biases feature extreme deviations, leading to high estimation variance, severe overestimation, and training instability. Current methods, which typically focus on reducing the mean or variance of bias while neglecting its underlying distributional properties, remain insufficient in mitigating the impact of these heavy-tailed deviations. Consequently, there is a pressing need to explicitly model and mitigate the heavy-tailed nature of Q-value biases to ensure robust value estimation and stable policy improvement during the online transition.
