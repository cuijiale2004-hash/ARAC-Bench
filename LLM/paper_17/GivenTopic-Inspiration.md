Given Topic: The field concerns test-time composition of large foundation models. While micro-level fusion via parameter merging faces fundamental constraints from architectural heterogeneity and closed-source APIs, macro-level coordination of diverse models remains underdeveloped. Existing approaches to multi-model orchestration predominantly rely on static routing heuristics or expensive parallel inference, lacking adaptive contextual understanding. The core challenge lies in designing an efficient coordination mechanism that can dynamically harness complementary capabilities across heterogeneous models without weight modification, while operating under strict inference budget constraints. Specifically, how to extract sufficient contextual signals from lightweight representations to enable effective, turn-based delegation among diverse agents remains an open scientific question.
