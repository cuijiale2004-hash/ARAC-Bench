Given Topic: The field of multimodal learning aims to equip vision-language models with the capacity to interpret sequential visual data for interactive and embodied applications. However, prevailing training paradigms predominantly emphasize static image comprehension or unordered multi-image alignment, lacking systematic exposure to fine-grained temporal dynamics and procedural logic. Consequently, smaller deployable variants—critical for edge-based robotics, navigation, and interface automation—exhibit profound deficiencies in tracking state changes, anticipating action consequences, and verifying procedural consistency. The fundamental bottleneck stems from the absence of large-scale, procedurally coherent training data derived from authentic sequential actions. The core scientific challenge is therefore to bridge the chasm between static visual comprehension and structured procedural reasoning by cultivating robust temporal understanding in resource-constrained multimodal systems.
