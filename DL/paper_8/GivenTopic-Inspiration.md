Given Topic: The heterogeneous nature of modern deployment environments necessitates adaptive accuracy-efficiency tradeoffs for deep neural networks. Conventional neural architecture search is typically confined to a single design space and incurs substantial training costs, whereas many-to-many composition paradigms that reuse pretrained models via internal layer stitching offer a compelling alternative. Nevertheless, prevailing stitch configuration strategies depend on rigid heuristics—such as linking architecturally adjacent scales or assuming layer-wise parity—that limit cross-family generalization and frequently yield suboptimal tradeoff curves. Concurrently, standard representational similarity metrics exhibit weak predictive power for stitching success. The fundamental challenge is to establish a principled, automated, and architecture-agnostic criterion for identifying compatible intermediate layers across pretrained networks, enabling dense and efficient interpolation along the accuracy-efficiency spectrum.

Given Inspiration:
1. The empirical observation that proximity-based heuristics for layer stitching systematically overlook high-quality configurations, indicating that architectural adjacency is an unreliable proxy for cross-network compatibility.
2. The duality of stitching viability, which simultaneously demands representational alignability to facilitate lightweight transformation and functional consistency to maintain task performance.
3. The perspective from information theory that statistical divergence between intermediate activation distributions can jointly characterize geometric and decision-boundary compatibility across distinct networks.
4. The concept that a recipient layer’s inherent capacity to remap its inputs correlates with its ability to absorb and correct distributional discrepancies introduced by foreign source layers.
5. The insight that lightweight auxiliary classifiers applied to hidden layers can expose functional behavior, thereby enabling compatibility scoring without instantiating actual stitch layers.
6. The motivation to transcend monolithic supernetwork training by dynamically composing heterogeneous pretrained anchors into a continuous deployment-ready frontier.
