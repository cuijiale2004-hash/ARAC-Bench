Given Topic: The research field concerns enhancing the reasoning capabilities of large language models through parallel thinking mechanisms, where multiple reasoning branches are explored concurrently rather than sequentially. Current mainstream approaches predominantly rely on supervised fine-tuning with synthetic data, which encourages superficial imitation and teacher-forced learning rather than genuine exploration and generalization. While inference-time strategies can superficially elicit parallel behaviors, they fail to internalize these capabilities within the model parameters. Furthermore, applying reinforcement learning to instill adaptive parallel thinking faces a severe cold-start problem, as autoregressive models lack native priors for generating structured parallel trajectories, and existing reward formulations struggle to balance the encouragement of parallel behaviors against the optimization of final answer correctness. The core scientific problem is to develop a scalable training paradigm that enables models to autonomously acquire adaptive parallel thinking for complex real-world reasoning tasks while resolving the tension between structural exploration and outcome accuracy.
