Given Topic: The research field is real-world video super-resolution, which aims to reconstruct high-resolution video sequences from low-resolution observations degraded by unknown real-world factors. Current diffusion-based methods are capable of generating rich, realistic details, yet their reliance on iterative sampling incurs substantial computational overhead and slow inference. Recent efforts toward single-step generation and model compression have mitigated this cost for image tasks; however, extending such compression to video restoration introduces a severe tension between spatial detail fidelity and temporal coherence. Under aggressive parameter reduction, standard distillation paradigms entangle these competing objectives, leading to either flickering artifacts or over-smoothed outputs. The core scientific problem is therefore how to radically compress generative video restoration networks while decoupling and jointly optimizing the contradictory requirements of perceptual detail richness and inter-frame temporal consistency.

1. The observation that video restoration tasks inherently differ from unconditional video generation because low-resolution inputs already encode substantial structural layout and temporal continuity, implying that global spatio-temporal dependency modeling may be disproportionately heavy relative to the actual information that must be inferred.
2. The recognition that spatial detail enrichment and temporal consistency are fundamentally competing objectives in generative video restoration, as the former requires encouraging high-frequency pixel variations while the latter requires suppressing cross-frame discrepancies.
3. The hypothesis that adversarial supervision can be decomposed into disentangled assessment criteria rather than a monolithic real-versus-fake signal, allowing a compressed model to receive independent gradient guidance for detail realism and temporal stability.
4. The insight that maintaining temporal coherence is intrinsically less demanding than synthesizing fine spatial de novo details, suggesting that temporal modeling can be handled by lightweight mechanisms while spatial synthesis retains a stronger backbone capacity.
5. The realization that exact regression between a massive teacher and a heavily compressed student of divergent architecture is infeasible, motivating a distillation paradigm that relaxes point-wise fidelity in favor of adversarial distribution alignment.
