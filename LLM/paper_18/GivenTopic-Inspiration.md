While scaling inference-time computation through lengthy chain-of-thought processes significantly enhances model performance on complex tasks, prevailing architectures incur quadratic computational costs and linear memory overheads with respect to sequence length. The core scientific challenge lies in decoupling reasoning efficiency from sequence length reduction—enabling sustained computational scalability without compromising reasoning capacity, while simultaneously mitigating error accumulation caused by low-quality intermediate reasoning steps.
