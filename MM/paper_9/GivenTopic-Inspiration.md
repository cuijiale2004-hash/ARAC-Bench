Consequently, current systems lack the capacity for genuine compositional reasoning across multiple spatial regions and struggle to resolve contextual ambiguities intrinsic to complex scenes. The core scientific issue is how to achieve precise, contextually grounded understanding of arbitrary visual regions while simultaneously modeling their dynamic interactions within broader scene semantics, ultimately shifting from passive description to active, dialogue-driven reasoning.
