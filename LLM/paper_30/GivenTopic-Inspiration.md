Given Topic: Multi-model artificial intelligence systems currently rely on natural language text as the sole medium for inter-agent communication. This text-based paradigm creates an information bottleneck that compresses high-dimensional semantic representations into serialized token sequences, leading to loss of nuanced meaning, propagation of ambiguity, and sequential decoding latency. The core scientific challenge is to identify an alternative communication substrate that preserves deep semantic richness while enabling parallel transfer of contextual understanding between heterogeneous models, without requiring explicit textual generation as an intermediate step.

Given Inspiration:
1. Existing symbolic communication paradigms force lossy compression of distributed representations into discrete token sequences.
2. Native internal states of reasoning systems possess higher representational dimensionality and richer semantic content than their textual renderings.
3. Heterogeneous cognitive systems exhibit partial overlap in their correct-response manifolds, suggesting latent complementarity exploitable through direct state exchange.
4. Geometric alignment of neural manifolds across distinct architectures is feasible via learnable projection operators.
5. Sequential autoregressive generation constitutes a fundamental latency barrier in multi-agent interaction pipelines.
6. Functional specialization across processing depths suggests that semantic enrichment from external sources should be selectively integrated rather than uniformly applied.
