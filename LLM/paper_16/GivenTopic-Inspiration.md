Given Topic: The research lies in the domain of large language model unlearning, where the goal is to selectively erase undesired knowledge while preserving general capabilities. A critical bottleneck is that existing unlearning effects are fragile and readily neutralized by post-deployment weight perturbations, including model compression and fine-tuning. Prior robust unlearning paradigms predominantly address this by reformulating loss objectives against anticipated attack modalities, leaving the intrinsic role of the optimization process unexplored. This work investigates the fundamental relationship between the information granularity of optimization algorithms and the stability of unlearning, seeking to improve resilience against weight-space tampering without attack-specific objective redesign or sacrificing forgetting efficacy.

Given Inspiration:
1. Empirical observations that heuristic adjustments to optimization configurations can unexpectedly improve resilience against weight perturbations, hinting at a deeper connection between optimizer properties and unlearning stability.
2. The spectrum of optimizer information granularity, spanning gradient-free estimates to full first-order gradients to second-order curvature, offers a principled axis to characterize optimization fidelity.
3. The counterintuitive principle that degrading gradient precision can enhance robustness, as optimization noise may drive convergence toward wider loss basins that are inherently resistant to perturbations.
4. The mathematical equivalence between gradient-free methods and randomized smoothing, which naturally embeds perturbation tolerance into the optimization trajectory without objective-level modifications.
5. The inherent trade-off between high-precision optimization and noise tolerance suggests that robust unlearning requires reconciling accurate forgetting with resilient basin selection.
6. Game-theoretic optimization structures, where a precise follower process prepares conditions for a noise-injecting leader, provide a conceptual framework for balancing forgetting efficacy with robustness.
