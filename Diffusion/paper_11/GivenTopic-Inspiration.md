Given Topic: The research addresses digital watermarking for latent diffusion-based image generation. Existing post-hoc methods operate externally after synthesis, leaving them susceptible to removal and disconnected from the generative mechanism. Concurrent in-generation approaches typically demand costly retraining or fine-tuning of model components, often concentrating watermark energy in narrow frequency ranges that are easily degraded by standard distortions. Meanwhile, seed-based in-generation techniques can significantly perturb semantic content and lack rigorous statistical control over false alarms. The central problem is how to intrinsically embed watermarks during the diffusion sampling trajectory without modifying the pre-trained model, while preserving semantic fidelity, guaranteeing provably low false alarm rates, and inheriting or enhancing robustness against image transformations.

Given Inspiration:
1. The observation that diffusion sampling trajectories can be steered by external differentiable objectives through gradient-based guidance without altering the underlying generative model.
2. The perspective that a watermark decoder can function as a guiding signal during iterative generation, rather than serving solely as a post-hoc verification module.
3. The insight that training-time augmentations used to harden detectors can be translated into generation-time constraints to extend robustness beyond original specifications.
4. The theoretical view from side-informed communication that host content should be treated as known side information rather than as unstructured noise.
5. The recognition that generative AI lacks a user-perceptible "original" image, rendering traditional distortion metrics inadequate and shifting priority toward semantic stability.
6. The engineering motivation to avoid confining watermark energy to high-frequency reconstruction details and instead distribute it across the full spectral content of the image.
7. The desire to strike a balance between methods that drastically alter image composition and those that merely append invisible residuals, aiming for structural integration without semantic drift.
