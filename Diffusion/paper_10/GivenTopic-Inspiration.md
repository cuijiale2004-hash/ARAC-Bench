Given Topic: Multi-objective optimization aims to identify the Pareto front representing optimal trade-offs among conflicting objectives. However, conventional approaches—including evolutionary algorithms, scalarization methods, and gradient-based descent techniques—encounter substantial scalability limitations when addressing high-dimensional or computationally expensive problems. Moreover, algorithms tailored to specific settings such as offline or Bayesian optimization often sacrifice generalizability, resulting in fragmented methodological progress. Consequently, a central scientific challenge lies in developing a unified, scalable framework capable of simultaneously ensuring convergence toward Pareto-optimal solutions and maintaining diverse coverage of the front across heterogeneous problem contexts without problem-specific architectural redesign.
