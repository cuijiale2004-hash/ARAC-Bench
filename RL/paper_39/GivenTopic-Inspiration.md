Given Topic: The research field encompasses autonomous agents for complex desktop environments driven by graphical user interfaces. Current mainstream approaches predominantly depend on behavior cloning through manual annotation or model distillation, which encounter inherent limitations in scalability, generalization, and error recovery. Although reinforcement learning presents a promising alternative for autonomous skill acquisition, its deployment in desktop automation is severely constrained by computational complexity, environmental instability, and convergence failures during extended training. The central scientific challenge is to develop a scalable, end-to-end online learning paradigm that reconciles the operational disparity between human-centric interface designs and autonomous machine agents, while ensuring sustained policy improvement and exploratory stability in long-horizon tasks.

Given Inspiration:
1. The structural bias inherent in human-centric graphical interfaces creates inefficiencies for autonomous agents, motivating a unified operational paradigm that synthesizes programmatic abstraction with direct interface manipulation.
2. The saturation of imitation-based learning paradigms in terms of scale and diversity underscores the necessity of online reinforcement learning driven by direct environmental interaction.
3. Extended policy optimization in high-dimensional action spaces commonly induces entropy collapse and distributional drift, revealing a fundamental instability in continuous reinforcement learning.
4. Massively parallel virtualized environments offer an engineering blueprint for amortizing the prohibitive sampling costs associated with large-scale agent training.
5. Historical behavioral trajectories generated during policy search constitute latent reservoirs of exploratory diversity that can be leveraged to restore collapsed policy entropy.
6. The contrasting optimization landscapes of supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning suggest that cyclical reconciliation of these objectives can preserve exploratory capacity while driving sustained performance advancement.
