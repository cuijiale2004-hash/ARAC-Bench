Given Topic: In the domain of knowledge-intensive language generation, contemporary systems frequently falter during multi-step reasoning despite retrieving relevant documents. Prevailing retrieval-augmented paradigms deliver information as unstructured text passages, forcing models to implicitly infer cross-passage relationships and logical bridges without explicit intermediate organization. This lack of structured scaffolding produces fragile reasoning trajectories and factual inconsistencies. Meanwhile, static, globally-constructed knowledge graphs, though relationally explicit, demand prohibitive offline indexing expenditure and necessarily subsume document-agnostic evidence that introduces ambiguity and contradiction. The fundamental challenge resides in enabling dynamic, inference-time structuring of retrieved evidence into query-aligned knowledge organizations that circumvent offline construction burdens while furnishing precise, coherent contextual support for complex reasoning.

Given Inspiration:
1. Empirical interpretability research suggesting that model hallucinations and reasoning failures frequently originate from breakdowns in implicit internal fact-chaining over unstructured contexts, highlighting the necessity of explicit intermediate knowledge structures.
2. The practical and economic constraints of static corpus-level knowledge graph construction, which necessitate massive offline computation and inevitably aggregate irrelevant or conflicting relations from heterogeneous sources.
3. The conceptual merit of question-specific knowledge assembly, where structured representations are dynamically instantiated from query-targeted documents to ensure contextual coherence and disambiguate contradictory evidence.
4. Human cognitive strategies of iterative inquiry and incremental knowledge refinement, wherein reasoning progresses through sequential identification of information deficiencies followed by targeted evidence acquisition.
5. The representational advantage of graph-structured semantics, which renders multi-hop dependencies and entity relationships explicitly navigable rather than latent within sequential text.
