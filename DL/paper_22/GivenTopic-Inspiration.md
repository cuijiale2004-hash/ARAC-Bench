Given Topic: The simulation of three-dimensional flexible solid deformation and contact represents a critical challenge in computational physics and engineering. While deep learning-based graph neural networks have emerged as promising alternatives to classical numerical solvers for their efficiency in handling unstructured meshes, prevailing paradigms predominantly rely on vertex-centric abstractions that reduce complex geometries to point-and-edge graphs. This simplification inherently neglects higher-dimensional geometric entities—specifically two-dimensional boundary facets and three-dimensional volumetric cells—that are intrinsic to the original mesh discretization. Consequently, existing approaches struggle to accurately represent boundary surfaces and volumetric characteristics, particularly under sparse mesh configurations where nodal sampling becomes insufficient for capturing localized physical fields. The core scientific problem lies in developing a geometrically faithful representation that integrates multi-dimensional mesh elements to enable precise boundary-aware contact modeling and stable volumetric information propagation under coarse discretization, without incurring the prohibitive computational costs of traditional high-resolution numerical methods.
