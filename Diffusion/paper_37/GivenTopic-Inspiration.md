Given Topic: The field of visual generative modeling focuses on latent diffusion paradigms that rely on variational autoencoders for dimensionality reduction. Existing approaches suffer from substantial computational costs in training and inference, often requiring extensive sampling steps to achieve satisfactory generation quality. A critical limitation lies in the structural properties of the latent space: current representations exhibit significant semantic entanglement and weak discriminative organization, which hampers the optimization dynamics of diffusion models and restricts transferability to broader vision tasks such as perception and understanding. The core problem is how to construct a unified latent space that simultaneously possesses clear semantic separability, rich perceptual detail, and cross-task generalizability, thereby enabling efficient generative training and few-step inference without relying on conventional variational autoencoder architectures.

Given Inspiration:
1. Analysis of latent space geometry reveals that semantic entanglement in existing compression spaces fundamentally impedes the convergence and sampling efficiency of diffusion processes.
2. Self-supervised visual representations demonstrate inherent semantic discriminability and class-level separation, suggesting their suitability as a foundational substrate for generative modeling.
3. The representational requirements for visual perception, understanding, and generation need not be disjoint; a unified feature space can potentially satisfy the structural demands of all three task categories.
4. Optimization landscapes in generative modeling are simplified when the underlying latent space exhibits clear inter-class boundaries and coherent intra-class structure, reducing the complexity of learned transition dynamics.
5. Faithful image reconstruction necessitates the preservation of both high-level semantic structure and low-level perceptual detail, which can be addressed through complementary rather than monolithic encoding strategies.
