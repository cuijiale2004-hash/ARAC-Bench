Given Topic: Text-guided image editing in generative visual models seeks to modify images according to natural language while preserving unedited content. Despite advances in spatial masking and attention control, existing methods exhibit a persistent asymmetry: modifications to foreground structures often succeed, whereas alterations to background and stylistic regions frequently fail or inadvertently degrade preserved content. Current paradigms predominantly frame editing as a spatial localization problem, partitioning images into binary edit and preserve zones. This overlooks the intrinsic structure of the guidance signal and its relationship to regional information density. The core scientific challenge lies in understanding how semantic scale is encoded within the model's internal directional signals, and how to achieve disentangled control over distinct semantic layers—such as object structure versus background atmosphere—without relying on external segmentation or rigid spatial constraints.
