Given Topic: Diffusion models empirically achieve remarkable sample efficiency, yet standard theoretical estimation bounds suffer from the curse of dimensionality by scaling inversely with ambient dimension. Recent structural assumptions—such as low-dimensional subspace priors or full-space mixture distributions—partially alleviate this but fail to simultaneously capture both the multi-manifold geometry and the local multi-modal distributional complexity observed in real-world data. Unimodal latent subspace models cannot represent multi-modality within individual manifolds, whereas unstructured mixture models ignore the low-dimensional support structure. This creates a critical gap in understanding how score-based generative models can efficiently learn distributions that are simultaneously concentrated on unions of subspaces and locally multi-modal. The core scientific problem is to establish a unified theoretical framework that escapes ambient dimensionality in estimation error and provides rigorous convergence guarantees for the associated non-convex score matching optimization under joint multi-subspace and multi-modal data assumptions.

Given Inspiration:
1. Empirical observations that natural image data concentrates near a union of distinct low-dimensional manifolds rather than a single global manifold.
2. The intrinsic multi-modality within individual local manifolds, which is not faithfully captured by unimodal latent priors.
3. The persistent inability of standard minimax bounds that scale with ambient dimension to explain the practical sample efficiency of diffusion models.
4. The universal approximation property of mixture distributions for representing complex smooth densities within structured low-dimensional latent spaces.
5. The structural insight that score functions of mixture distributions naturally decompose into localized terms each specializing to individual mixture components.
6. The geometric intuition that parameter estimation for well-separated mixture components may exhibit favorable local curvature properties near the ground truth.
7. The necessity of disentangling intrinsic data complexity, in terms of manifold and modal structure, from the high ambient dimensionality to obtain tight statistical guarantees.
