Given Topic: Masked diffusion language models generate text through iterative refinement, gradually revealing tokens over multiple steps. Existing decoding paradigms suffer from severe computational redundancy because each step uniformly recomputes attention and transformation operations across all sequence positions, including those whose values have already been determined and exhibit negligible further variation. This persistent per-step complexity throughout the entire sampling trajectory creates a substantial bottleneck for efficient inference and practical deployment. The core challenge is to devise a principled mechanism for identifying when individual positions have reached adequate stability during iterative refinement, enabling their disengagement from subsequent redundant computation without disrupting the coherence or fidelity of the generated output.

Given Inspiration:
1. Variable convergence rates in iterative systems: Individual elements within multi-step decoding processes frequently stabilize at disparate times, indicating that uniform global computation across all positions and iterations is inherently inefficient.
2. Spatial sparsity beyond temporal acceleration: Computational savings can be realized by exploiting spatial heterogeneity within each step—concentrating resources on dynamically evolving regions while treating stabilized regions as static context.
3. Contextual support without active propagation: Positions that have ceased evolving can continue to provide necessary contextual references for actively updating positions without requiring their own forward transformations to be repeatedly executed.
4. Local stationarity as a convergence diagnostic: The consistency of a position's posterior distribution across successive refinement steps offers a principled signal for determining when active computation at that location is no longer required.
5. Monotonic compute contraction: Permanently removing stabilized positions from the active compute set creates a progressively shrinking computational footprint that mirrors the diminishing uncertainty of the sequence as it solidifies.
